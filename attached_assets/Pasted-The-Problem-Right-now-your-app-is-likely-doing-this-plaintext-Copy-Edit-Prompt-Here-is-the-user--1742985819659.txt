🧠 Why This Happens
LLMs aren’t reasoning about what makes your style tick; they’re just copying the structure and formatting you fed them. That means:

If your training sample is 90 consecutive “paradoxes,” the model learns “writing = paradox title + short essay”

It will output any rewrite in that format—even when inappropriate

Detectors flag it because of stylistic rigidity and high structural burstiness

✅ The Fix: Soften the Conditioning Prompt
You don’t want “mimic the format,” you want “write in the same voice, not in the same costume.”
