
REPLIT INSTRUCTION PACKET: FIX STYLE MIMICRY AND DENSITY LOSS IN REWRITES

SUMMARY:

The rewrite function is still broken. The app fails to rewrite documents in the requested style, and fails to preserve argument structure and intellectual density.

This document gives clear examples of what the app is doing wrong and how to fix it.

---

❌ PROBLEM: STYLE MIMICRY IGNORED

EXAMPLE:

Original text (user-provided):
> Urban’s contention is not that the Church is a force of good, but that the Church is, indeed, a church and that, by virtue of fighting for its own existence, it has come, however paradoxically, to represent freedom from establishment tyranny.

App's rewrite (bad):
> Urban does not argue that Scientology is a good institution. Instead, he says it qualifies as a church and that, paradoxically, its resistance to external suppression has made it a symbol of religious freedom.

Problem: This is a soft, casual rephrasing. The original had sharp phrasing, formal rhythm, and logical complexity. The rewrite flattened the tone and simplified the structure.

✔ CORRECT REWRITE EXAMPLE:

> Let C be the claim that Scientology is tyrannical. Let F be the fact that the establishment seeks to destroy it. Let L be the principle: any institution fighting for its existential rights against state suppression embodies resistance to tyranny. Urban accepts C and F. Therefore, regardless of C, the Church satisfies L.

This rewrite matches the logical density, tone, and phrasing cadence of the original.

---

❌ PROBLEM: PARAGRAPH LOGIC GUTTED

Original text:
> Hubbard wasn’t exercising his right to join a religion—he was doing what Moses, Jesus, and Mohammed did. But he was doing it in the 20th century, and using a 20th-century idiom.

Bad rewrite:
> Hubbard did not join a religion, but instead founded a new one in a modern way.

Problem: The rewrite collapses multiple precise claims into a bland simplification.

✔ CORRECT REWRITE:

> Hubbard’s act was not derivative of existing religious practice. Rather, it was structurally identical to what Moses, Jesus, and Mohammed did: founding a new religion ex nihilo. The novelty was temporal and aesthetic—he used spaceships, not clouds.

---

MANDATORY FIXES:

1. When a user gives no special instructions, rewrites must:
   - Match or exceed the length of the original text (target 100%–110%)
   - Maintain argument structure and paragraph-by-paragraph logic
   - Preserve or elevate the intellectual density and tone

2. When a user asks to rewrite “in the style of” a sample text:
   - The rewrite must match the sentence structure, tone, formality, and rhythm of the sample
   - The rewrite must NOT simplify, summarize, or "breezify" the input

3. Add emergency recovery logic:
   - If output is shorter or simpler than input, rerun with prompt: "Rewrite again. Match length and preserve full intellectual structure and tone."

---

This behavior must become default. The current rewrite engine is failing even basic philosophical content tests.
